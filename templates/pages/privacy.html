Privacy Notice & Data Security Commitment

Last Updated: October 2026

1. Data Minimization & Privacy First

ScamShield is designed with privacy-first principles. We do not require registration to use the core scam checking tool. When you submit text or links for analysis, our engine processes the content in-memory to detect threat indicators. Full text content is not permanently saved by default; only truncated sanitized snippets and metric summaries are retained for personal history and statistical research.

2. What We Never Ask For

ScamShield will NEVER request passwords, One-Time Passwords (OTPs), bank account numbers, debit/credit card CVVs, or personal identity numbers. Please do not submit confidential financial credentials into any form.

3. User Controls

Registered users have full control over their scan history. You can view, inspect, or permanently delete your scan history records at any time from your personal dashboard.
